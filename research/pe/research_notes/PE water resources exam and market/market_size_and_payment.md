# PE Civil (WRE focus) market size and how PE prep is paid for

Research date: 2026-10-30. Primary data comes from NCEES "Squared" annual reports (fiscal years run Oct 1 to Sep 30) and the live NCEES PE Civil page. I downloaded the Squared PDFs and read the tables directly; the numbers below are copied from them. Estimates are labeled **[ESTIMATE]**.

Squared sources:
- Squared 2025 (FY 2024-25, Oct 1 2024 to Sep 30 2025): https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025-Squared_access.pdf
- Squared 2024 (FY 2023-24): https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Squared-2024_pages.pdf
- Squared 2023 (FY 2022-23): https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Squared-2023_spreads.pdf
- NCEES PE Civil page (rolling six-month pass rates): https://ncees.org/pe-exam/civil/

## 1. PE Civil pass rates and examinee counts by depth (2023-2026)

### Takeaway
NCEES publishes counts as well as rates. **Water Resources and Environmental (WRE) is the largest PE Civil depth and is growing.** It had 5,697 attempts in FY 2024-25 (4,313 first-time plus 1,384 repeat), about 30% of all PE Civil attempts. It also has the highest first-time pass rate (71%). The latest six-month window, Jan-Jun 2026, had 3,496 WRE attempts, which annualizes to roughly 7,000.

### Cited Findings
**FY 2024-25 (Oct 2024 to Sep 2025), all takers.** Source: [NCEES Squared 2025](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025-Squared_access.pdf), PE pass-rate tables pp. 16-18.

| Depth | 1st-time volume | 1st-time pass | Repeat volume | Repeat pass | Total attempts |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Construction | 1,565 | 62% | 716 | 40% | 2,281 |
| Geotechnical | 782 | 65% | 360 | 42% | 1,142 |
| Structural | 2,879 | 61% | 1,314 | 42% | 4,193 |
| Transportation | 3,677 | 59% | 1,783 | 42% | 5,460 |
| **WRE** | **4,313** | **71%** | **1,384** | **50%** | **5,697** |
| Civil total | 13,216 | | 5,557 | | 18,773 |

- WRE takers with an EAC/ABET bachelor's degree: 3,688 first-time at 71%, 1,146 repeat at 51%. "Other takers" (no ABET degree or none reported): 571 first-time at 73%, 208 repeat at 47%. Source: [Squared 2025](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025-Squared_access.pdf)

**FY 2023-24 (Oct 2023 to Sep 2024).** Source: [Squared 2024](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Squared-2024_pages.pdf)
- Construction 1,793 first-time at 55%, 815 repeat at 39%
- Geotechnical 899 at 58%, 401 at 40%
- Structural 3,194 at 57%, 1,333 at 39%
- Transportation 3,860 at 63%, 1,393 at 43%
- **WRE 4,246 first-time at 68%, 1,240 repeat at 51%, 5,486 total**
- Civil total: 19,174 attempts

**FY 2022-23 (Oct 2022 to Sep 2023).** Source: [Squared 2023](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Squared-2023_spreads.pdf)
- Construction 1,635 at 49%, 608 repeat at 32%
- Geotechnical 849 at 52%, 274 at 34%
- Structural 2,813 at 58%, 821 at 38%
- Transportation 3,529 at 63%, 882 at 44%
- **WRE 3,573 first-time at 65%, 703 repeat at 44%, 4,276 total**
- Civil total: 15,687 attempts

**Rolling six-month windows (NCEES PE Civil page).** Source: [NCEES PE Civil](https://ncees.org/pe-exam/civil/)
- Jan-Jun 2026 (page marked "Last updated July 2026"):
  - Construction 1,000 first-time at 60%, 417 repeat at 38%
  - Geotechnical 434 at 63%, 201 at 44%
  - Structural 1,624 at 62%, 847 at 43%
  - Transportation 1,929 at 61%, 1,097 at 43%
  - **WRE 2,620 at 71%, 876 repeat at 49%**
  - These are pass rates "for the January-June or July-December population."
- Jul-Dec 2025 (the version marked "last updated January 2026," seen only in a search snippet of the same page): **WRE 2,264 first-time, 811 repeat.** I did not see the full table for this window.
- Exam facts: $400 fee paid to NCEES, plus any state board fee. Computer-based, taken year-round at Pearson centers. 80 questions, 9-hour appointment. Source: [NCEES PE Civil](https://ncees.org/pe-exam/civil/)

**Older point for context:** NSPE, citing NCEES, gave about 3,173 first-time WRE takers and a 70% pass rate for Oct 2016 to Sep 2017. Source: [NSPE PE magazine, July 2018](https://www.nspe.org/career-growth/pe-magazine/july-2018/most-popular-pe-exams-pass-rates). I saw this only in a search snippet, not the full article.

### Inferences
- WRE attempts by fiscal year: 4,276, then 5,486, then 5,697. That is +33% over two years.
  - Its share of PE Civil attempts went from 27.3% to 28.6% to 30.3%.
  - Jul 2025 to Jun 2026 adds up to 3,075 + 3,496 = **6,571 WRE attempts**, about 15% above FY 2024-25. **[Derived from NCEES numbers. The two windows do not line up with the fiscal year.]**
- In FY 2024-25 WRE was the largest PE Civil depth, ahead of Transportation (5,460). It also overtook Transportation in first-time takers in FY 2023-24.
- WRE repeat takers pass only about 50% of the time, against 71% for first-timers. About 1,400 people a year retake WRE and are probably the most motivated buyers of prep.
- Total PE Civil attempts fell slightly in FY 2024-25 (19,174 to 18,773) while WRE grew. Construction and Geotechnical shrank.
- Attempts are not the same as unique people. Someone who fails twice in a year counts twice, so unique WRE candidates a year are probably somewhat below attempts. **[ESTIMATE: roughly 5,000-5,500 unique people in FY 2024-25.]**

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve the full NCEES Jul-Dec 2025 table for every depth, only the WRE row from a snippet.
- NCEES does not publish counts by calendar year, only by fiscal year and six-month window.

## 2. FE Civil volume and FE-to-PE conversion

### Takeaway
About 26,500 FE Civil attempts were taken in FY 2024-25 (16,639 first-time). NCEES publishes no figure for how many FE passers later take the PE. A rough cross-sectional ratio puts PE Civil first-time takers at about 0.8 per FE Civil first-time taker, with a lag of about four years.

### Cited Findings
- FE Civil FY 2024-25: 16,639 first-time at 61%, 9,863 repeat at 32%. Source: [Squared 2025](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025-Squared_access.pdf)
  - EAC/ABET-degree takers: 12,256 first-time at 64%, 7,275 repeat at 34%
  - "Other" takers: 4,069 first-time at 53%
- FE Civil FY 2023-24: 15,619 first-time at 60%, 9,059 repeat at 33%. Source: [Squared 2024](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Squared-2024_pages.pdf)
- FE Civil FY 2022-23: 15,017 first-time at 60%, 8,042 repeat at 32%. Source: [Squared 2023](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Squared-2023_spreads.pdf)
- FE Environmental, a separate FE exam that also feeds WRE candidates: 2,543 first-time at 67% and 1,092 repeat in FY 2024-25. Source: [Squared 2025](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025-Squared_access.pdf)
- NCEES says examinees with about four years of experience after graduation have the best PE pass rates. Rates drop in proportion to the distance from that four-year mark (2014-25 data). Source: [Squared 2025](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025-Squared_access.pdf)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE]** FE Civil first-time takers (about 16.6k) times a 61% first-time pass rate, plus repeat passers (9,863 × 32% ≈ 3.2k), gives about 13k FE Civil passes a year.
  - PE Civil first-time takers were 13.2k in FY 2024-25.
  - Most FE Civil passers therefore appear to reach the PE eventually. This is a rough steady-state comparison, not a tracked cohort, and some of these people hold PE Environmental or other credentials.
- For FE for Raccoons, this means **the FE user base is a natural PE pipeline with a lag of about four years.** A student who passes the FE today is a PE buyer around 2030.

### Gaps
- I found no NCEES longitudinal (cohort) FE-to-PE conversion rate, and Squared does not report one.

## 3. Total PE examinees (all disciplines) and trend

### Takeaway
Total PE examinees are about 30k a year and flat to slightly up. There were 29,838 in FY 2024-25 and 29,359 in FY 2023-24. PE Civil is about 63% of all PE attempts.

### Cited Findings
- FY 2024-25 examinee totals: FE 57,902, FS 2,286, **PE 29,838**, PS 1,825, PE Structural 2,018, total 93,869. Source: [Squared 2025](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025-Squared_access.pdf)
- FY 2023-24 totals: FE 53,213, **PE 29,359**, PS 1,584, PE Structural 2,460. Source: [Squared 2024](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Squared-2024_pages.pdf)
  - NCEES called it "the most PE exams since 2018." The last pencil-and-paper exam was Oct 2023, and all exams have been computer-based since April 2024.
- FY 2022-23: PE 24,710 (computer-based column). Source: [Squared 2023](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Squared-2023_spreads.pdf)
- FE volume by fiscal year: 38,211 (2014-15), 51,814 (2018-19), 38,396 (2019-20, COVID), 47,917 (2022-23), 53,213 (2023-24), 57,902 (2024-25). Source: [Squared 2025](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025-Squared_access.pdf)
- U.S. engineering licenses (counted per state, so one engineer licensed in three states counts three times): 1,021,124 in 2025, up from 986,562 in 2024. Source: [Squared 2025](https://ncees.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/2025-Squared_access.pdf)

### Inferences
- PE Civil share of all PE attempts: 18,773 / 29,838 = **63%** in FY 2024-25.
- FE volume rose about 21% over two years (47.9k to 57.9k). With a roughly four-year lag, this points to PE Civil volume rising later in the decade. **[ESTIMATE]**

### Gaps
- FY 2022-23 PE total may undercount because of the paper-to-computer transition. Treat 24,710 cautiously.

## 4. Market size for PE prep spending (bottom-up)

### Takeaway
**[ESTIMATE]** PE Civil WRE prep spending is roughly **$2-9M a year**. All of PE Civil is roughly **$6-28M**, and all PE disciplines about $9-45M. A low-price product does not need much of this. 500 WRE buyers at $99 is only about $50k a year.

### Cited Findings (price points used in the assumptions)
- **PPI (Kaplan), PE Civil WRE, Oct 2026:** [PPI WRE page](https://ppi2pass.com/pe-exam/civil/water-resources-environmental-depth)
  - Live Online bundle $2,595
  - OnDemand $545 a month, $1,595 for 3 months, $2,295 for 6 months
  - "Learning Hub" $695 for 3 months, $895 for 6 months
  - Tutoring plus money-back-guarantee bundle $3,795
- PPI's live online PE Civil Construction course was listed at $2,095, down from $2,595, for 2026 sessions. Source: [PPI Construction](https://ppi2pass.com/pe-exam/civil/construction-depth) (seen via search snippet)
- **School of PE:**
  - The Kimley-Horn discounted PE course rate is $800 ([School of PE / Kimley-Horn page](https://schoolofpe.com/pages/kimley-horn-registration), © 2026).
  - Retail PE Civil prices quoted by third parties disagree: about $1,700 ([TestMasters comparison 2026](https://www.testmasters.com/compare/testmasters-vs-school-of-pe-vs-prepfe-civil-pe-exam-prep-comparison-2026-8c44a3/)), $1,990, and a $625 sale (third-party review sites). **Unverified.** The official page did not load (HTTP 429).
- Civil Engineering Academy "Ultimate Civil PE" course: about $997 (third-party listing, unverified). Source: [testprepinsight](https://testprepinsight.com/reviews/civil-engineering-academy-review/)
- Illinois Tech PE course: $1,195. Source: [IIT](https://www.iit.edu/opce/courses/engineering-and-fepe-exam-prep)
- NCEES exam fee: $400 per attempt. Source: [NCEES PE Civil](https://ncees.org/pe-exam/civil/)

### Inferences: bottom-up model **[ALL ESTIMATES]**

Assumptions:
- Attempts per year: WRE 5,700 (FY 2024-25) to 6,600 (latest 12 months). All PE Civil 18,800. All PE 29,800.
- Blended spend per attempt on prep, covering everyone from those who buy only the NCEES practice exam and handbook to those who buy a $2.6k live course:
  - Low $300
  - Mid $800 (roughly a mix of about 40% on a large course and 60% on self-study)
  - High $1,500

| Segment | Attempts | Low ($300) | Mid ($800) | High ($1,500) | Exam fees ($400) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PE Civil WRE | 5,700 | $1.7M | $4.6M | $8.6M | $2.3M |
| PE Civil WRE (latest 12 mo) | 6,600 | $2.0M | $5.3M | $9.9M | $2.6M |
| All PE Civil | 18,800 | $5.6M | $15.0M | $28.2M | $7.5M |
| All PE | 29,800 | $8.9M | $23.8M | $44.7M | $11.9M |

What this means for a $35-49 style product **[ESTIMATE]**:
- Reaching 10% of WRE attempts (about 600 a year) at $99 is about $60k a year.
- At $199 it is about $120k a year.
- Repeat takers (about 1,400 a year in WRE, 50% pass rate) are a high-intent sub-segment.

### Gaps
- I found no source for the actual share of PE candidates who buy a paid course versus self-study. The low/mid/high blended spend is an assumption.
- I could not verify School of PE's current official PE Civil retail price (HTTP 429).

## 5. Employer reimbursement and group/corporate licenses

### Takeaway
Employers paying for the PE is the norm in consulting firms. The only systematic survey found (Zweig Group, reported in 2015) says 89% of A/E firms pay exam and registration fees and 47% give a raise or bonus on passing. Prep-course reimbursement is common but capped; Kimley-Horn's documented cap is $1,500 lifetime for review courses. **The big prep companies sell corporate programs.** They mostly do not publish prices, which is a sign of a sales-led enterprise channel.

### Cited Findings
- **Zweig Group survey** (architecture, engineering, planning and environmental consulting firms; survey year not stated; article Sep/Oct 2015). Source: [NSPE PE Magazine, "A Goal and an Assist"](https://www.nspe.org/node/11703)
  - 89% of firms pay for "professional registration exams and professional registration fees"
  - 47% automatically give raises or cash bonuses for passing a first exam
  - 75% offer tuition or fee reimbursement (92% of full-service engineering firms)
  - 54% let staff attend classes during work hours
  - The survey gave no specific figure for prep courses.
- Firm examples in the same article (2015): [NSPE](https://www.nspe.org/node/11703)
  - Maser Consulting pays for a prep course and the exam, gives time off on exam day, and pays a pass bonus.
  - Merrick & Co. pays the PE exam, gives a raise on passing, and pays license fees.
- **Kimley-Horn (current, © 2026):** Source: [School of PE Kimley-Horn registration page](https://schoolofpe.com/pages/kimley-horn-registration)
  - Each employee has a "Licensure Allotment" with a **$1,500 cumulative lifetime maximum for review courses**.
  - The negotiated School of PE PE course rate is $800 (FE $585), deducted from that allotment through prepaid seats.
  - Anything above the allotment is paid personally.
- **City of Los Angeles Bureau of Engineering:** reimburses FE, PE and SE exam course costs **up to $1,000 a year**. Source: [LA BOE](https://dpw.lacity.gov/bureau-engineering-employee-development-opportunities)
- **USDA NRCS Iowa (federal, 2025 document):** reimburses FE/PE exam fees, prep-course tuition and books/reference materials. Source: [NRCS IA250-389](https://nrcs-prod.azureedge.us/sites/default/files/2025-12/IA250-389%20%20Misc%20Reimb%20for%20Official%20Bus%20Expense__FINAL.pdf)
- Counter-evidence: some engineers on Eng-Tips report employers that refuse to pay for study materials, or pay the exam but not prep. This is anecdotal. Source: [Eng-Tips](https://www.eng-tips.com/threads/pe-exam-prep.355282)
- **School of PE corporate program:** [School of PE partnerships](https://partnerships.schoolofpe.com/engineering-firm-pe-exam-prep)
  - Claims 200+ corporate partners and "50% of the ENR Top 100"
  - Named partners: Kimley-Horn, Bowman, Barge Design Solutions, Colliers Engineering & Design
  - Pricing is cohort-based or per-seat, with rollover seats and multi-year contracts. No list prices are published.
  - Includes an admin portal, quarterly outcome reports and a pass guarantee with free retake.
- **PPI (Kaplan) corporate program:** "transparent tiered" volume pricing that is not published, plus centralized billing, an account manager and admin reporting on practice exams. Covers FE, PE and SE. Source: [PPI Businesses](https://ppi2pass.com/businesses)
  - PPI-Kaplan per-person corporate access is also offered through Engineering Management Institute. Source: [EMI](https://engineeringmanagementinstitute.org/?p=22613)

### Inferences
- **Who pays shapes how to sell.** For many PE candidates the firm pays, often up to a cap ($1,000-1,500 in the examples found).
  - A $35-49 product falls below most reimbursement caps. It is also cheap enough that the candidate may just pay personally without filing a reimbursement.
  - So **individual card purchases remain viable.** The firm channel would mean invoicing, per-seat licenses, admin progress reports and a receipt suitable for an expense report.
- The incumbents' negotiated corporate rates (for example $800 a seat) show a firm-level price anchor far above $49. A low-cost product could position as a "supplement under the cap," or as a cheap tool firms can give everyone.
- Public agencies (cities, state DOTs, federal NRCS) also reimburse. WRE candidates are heavily public-sector and water-utility.

### Gaps
- I found no current (2023-2026) national survey of the share of employers paying for PE prep courses specifically. The Zweig figure is from 2015 or earlier and covers exam fees, not prep.
- I did not find HDR, Stantec, AECOM, Jacobs or Kiewit public benefit pages stating a PE prep policy. The searches returned nothing specific.
- Corporate per-seat prices for School of PE and PPI are not published, apart from the Kimley-Horn $800 figure.

## 6. Salary uplift after PE (motivation)

### Takeaway
ASCE's 2026 salary report says the median income for PE licensees is **nearly $49,000 a year higher** than for those with no license or certification. That is up from about $40k (2025) and about $42k (2024).

### Cited Findings
- **ASCE 2026 Civil Engineering Salary Report** (article Oct 8 2026, 2,444 ASCE members surveyed): Source: [ASCE](https://www.asce.org/publications-and-news/civil-engineering-source/article/2026/10/08/civil-engineering-salaries-continue-to-rise-as-industry-evolves)
  - Average base salary $152,000, up more than $8,000 from 2025
  - Entry-level average just above $77,000
  - PE licensees' median income is nearly $49,000 higher than for those with no licenses or certifications
- **ASCE 2025 report:** licensed civil engineers earn about $40,000 more than those without a license. Average base about $148,000. Source: [ASCE, Oct 14 2025](https://www.asce.org/publications-and-news/civil-engineering-source/article/2025/10/14/new-asce-report-shows-civil-engineering-salaries-up-an-average-9000); [Construction Equipment Guide](https://www.constructionequipmentguide.com/civil-engineering-salary-growth-outpaces-overall-workforce-according-to-new-report/69324)
  - The $40k figure is from search summaries; I did not read the full article.
- **ASCE 2024 report:** licensed engineers earn about $140,000, nearly $42,000 more than unlicensed. Source: [ASCE, Sep 26 2024](https://www.asce.org/publications-and-news/civil-engineering-source/article/2024/09/26/civil-engineering-salaries-rising-report-finds-but-should-they-be-even-higher)
  - This figure is from a search summary.

### Inferences
- The license-versus-no-license gap partly reflects experience, because licensees are older. It is not a pure causal effect of passing the PE.
- Even so, the stakes (tens of thousands of dollars a year) dwarf any prep price. That supports pricing above FE levels, and supports messaging around "pass first time" rather than "cheap."

### Gaps
- ASCE does not break out the PE premium by discipline, so there is no WRE-specific figure. Full reports are paywalled for members.
