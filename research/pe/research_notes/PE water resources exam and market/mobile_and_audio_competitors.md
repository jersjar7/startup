# Do PE competitors have mobile apps or audio? (2026-10-31)

Method: Apple's App Store and Podcasts search API (US), Google Play search
pages, and each seller's home page. Ratings and dates as of 2026-10-31.
Google Play was only searched by name, so Android coverage is partial.

## Audio

No one sells topic audio lessons for the PE Civil WRE.

- The Civil Engineering Academy Podcast (362 episodes, active): interviews and
  career stories, not topic lessons.
- Mechanical PE Exam Prep Podcast (44 episodes): pass stories and strategy.
- Civil Engineering Made Simple (412 episodes, daily): summaries of civil
  engineering books, not exam prep.
- "FE exam in the car" (6 episodes, started October 2026): exactly the commute
  lesson idea, for the FE (trusses, statics, statistics), including "Recognize
  the problem before you reach for the formula". Tiny and new.
- NCEES's own podcast "Advance" (17 episodes) stopped in January 2024.

## What it means

- Having an app is not unique: School of PE and PPI have one each, and a wave
  of small apps launched in 2026. But the big sellers' apps are poorly rated
  companions, and the small ones have no users yet.
- Nobody pairs a good phone app with commute audio for the WRE exam. That
  pairing is the open space, and someone has started the same idea for the FE.
