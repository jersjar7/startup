# PE Civil: Water Resources and Environmental

Research started 2026-10-30 on the `pe-research` branch. Two questions:
Jerson's own PE exam, and whether FE for Raccoons should offer a PE product.

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `reports/PE water resources exam and market.md` | The full cited research report: exam, eligibility, market, competitors, verdict |
| `study-plan-jerson.md` | Jerson's 18-week plan to sit the exam in March 2027 |
| `product-proposal.md` | The product proposal: waitlist first, then a paid piece decided in March 2027 (the $99 exam idea was dropped on 2026-10-31: competitors already sell timed exams for less) |
| `sample-problems-wre.md` | Six draft problems in the FE for Raccoons format, answers checked by calculation |
| `research_notes/PE water resources exam and market/` | The four source note files behind the report, every claim linked |

Nothing here is built into the product. Every build step waits on Jerson's
decisions, listed at the end of `product-proposal.md`.
