# FE Exam Market Size Research

**Date:** March 2026

## Total Annual Examinees (All Disciplines)

| Fiscal Year | Examinees |
|---|---|
| 2014-15 | 38,211 |
| 2015-16 | 43,648 |
| 2016-17 | 46,228 |
| 2017-18 | 48,128 |
| 2018-19 | 38,396 |
| 2019-20 | 48,746 |
| 2020-21 | 44,079 |
| 2021-22 | 47,917 |
| 2022-23 | 53,213 |
| **2024-25** | **57,902** |

The trend is clearly upward — nearly 58,000 examinees in the most recent year, up ~50% from a decade ago.

## Discipline Breakdown (2024-25)

| Discipline | Examinees | YoY Growth |
|---|---|---|
| FE Civil | ~26,500 | +7% |
| FE Mechanical | ~13,670 | +11% |
| FE Electrical & Computer | ~7,900 | +14% |
| Chemical, Environmental, Industrial, Other | ~9,800 (remainder) | - |

FE Civil is the largest discipline, accounting for roughly 46% of all FE examinees.

## Pass Rates by Discipline (2025)

| Discipline | Pass Rate |
|---|---|

| FE Mechanical | 73% |
| FE Chemical | 70% |
| FE Civil | ~68% |
| FE Industrial & Systems | 66% |

## Competitor Pricing

- PPI/Kaplan: $200-$800+ for FE prep courses
- PrepFE: ~$40/month
- FE for Raccoons target: $5-$10/account (significantly lower barrier to entry)

## Key Takeaways

- ~58,000 new potential users every year and growing
- These are people actively studying and willing to pay for prep materials
- Our price point is well below competitors, making it accessible to more students
- FE Civil alone has ~26,500 examinees/year — strong starting market for MVP
- NCEES administers FE exams globally, expanding the addressable market internationally
- The market has shown consistent growth over the past decade
