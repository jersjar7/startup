# App Review notes

Paste the block below into App Store Connect → the version → **App Review
Information → Notes**. The demo account credentials live in
`secrets/app-review-login.json` (gitignored, never committed).

Written for somebody who has minutes, not hours. Every line answers a question
a reviewer would otherwise have to find out by poking, or would reject over.

---

FE for Raccoons is a free study tool for the NCEES Fundamentals of
Engineering (FE) Civil exam, used by engineering students and working
engineers preparing for licensure.

SIGNING IN
The app requires an account to show study content. Please use the demo
account provided in the fields above. It is a normal account with some
progress on it, so the home screen shows a real state rather than an
empty one.

WHAT THE APP DOES
Short practice rounds organised into the 15 chapters of the FE Civil
exam specification. Tap Study, choose a chapter, and tap the play button
to begin. Everything is reachable from the two tabs at the bottom.

PURCHASES
There are none. The app is free, contains no in-app purchases, no
subscriptions and no advertising, and never asks for payment details.

NOTIFICATIONS
Study reminders are LOCAL notifications scheduled on the device. There is
no push server and no remote notification capability. They are off by
default and only requested if you turn on "Study reminders" in the
account sheet, so you will not be prompted for permission unless you go
looking for it.

THE WEBSITE
The app pairs with fe4raccoons.com, the same product on a larger screen.
The "Open the website" button in the account sheet opens it in the
browser. The website is also free; nothing in the app links to a purchase.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Profile icon (top right) → Delete account. It asks for the password and
removes the account and all its data immediately.
