The finishing moment, measured from these recordings (30 fps frames,
ffmpeg). t0 is the next platform starting to rise, fitted to its measured
face width against the rise curve; every time below is from t0, in ms.
Fill and line are eased in and start under the platform lip, so they are
measured at their visible half way point (spec: fill 150-1200 -> 675,
line 400-1200 -> 800), give or take a frame.

newly reached lesson  1800-2100   first seen 1830, full 1997 (day);
                                  1943 / 2010 (night, faint at first on
                                  the dark ground); 1844 / 2044 (tablet)

The tile starts growing at 1800 with the bounce and eases out over 300 ms;
it stays on the finished lesson until then.

Reduce Motion (phone): the finished platform's face fades in 180 ms
(6.03 to 6.20 s in the raw take), then the road, pin and tile change in
one cross-fade (6.23 to 6.40 s, about 180 ms). No pulse, bounce, rise or
ring sweep.

phone-*: iPhone 17e simulator (390 pt wide), debug build, below the
status bar. tablet-landscape: iPad Air 11-inch simulator drawn a quarter
turn round (lib/dev/road_moment.dart, TURN) and turned back, cropped to the
path pane. Each clip runs from just before the last round is played until
the next platform settles.
