The finishing moment, measured from these recordings (30 fps frames,
ffmpeg). t0 is the next platform starting to rise, fitted to its measured
face width against the rise curve; every time below is from t0, in ms.
Fill and line are eased in and start under the platform lip, so they are
measured at their visible half way point (spec: fill 150-1200 -> 675,
line 400-1200 -> 800), give or take a frame.

                     spec   phone day  phone night  tablet landscape
rise starts             0           0            0                 0
fill half way         675         591          663               620
line half way         800         766          752               773
pulse first glow     1200        1163         1164              1210
bounce starts        1800        1796         1798              1820
bounce peak         1.05x       1.048        1.047     about 1.05 (2 px rounding)
settled              2100        2096         2098              2120

Reduce Motion (phone): the road and pin change in one cross-fade over
6 frames (about 200 ms at 30 fps; spec 180 ms), no pulse, no bounce, no rise.

phone-*: iPhone 17e simulator (390 pt wide), debug build.
tablet-landscape: iPad Air 11-inch simulator drawn a quarter turn round
(lib/dev/road_moment.dart, TURN) and turned back, cropped to the path pane.
Each clip runs from just before the last round is played until the next
platform settles.
