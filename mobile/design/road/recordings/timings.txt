The finishing moment (owner, 2026-11-03: the pin walks the whole way).

Timeline, in ms from the next platform starting to rise:

  rise                       0 - 300
  fill                     150 - 1200
  line                     400 - 1200
  pin walk                1200 - 2100   old road, up the step, through the
                                        face centre, down, new road to rest;
                                        glow under the pin fades in and out
                                        over 150 ms at each end
  bounce + spring tile    2100 - 2400   tile moves to the new lesson as the
                                        pin arrives
  total                   2400

The real step up and down is the platform's height over the road slab:
6 pt on the phone, 7 pt on the tablet.

Reduce Motion (phone): no walk. The finished platform's face fades in,
then road, pin, glow-free rest spot and tile change in one 180 ms
cross-fade (about 1.57 to 1.73 s into the clip).
